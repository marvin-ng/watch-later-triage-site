Watch Later Triage

A Chrome extension that makes a huge YouTube Watch Later list searchable and manageable. Local-first: your list stays in your browser.

The problem

Watch Later keeps growing, YouTube gives you no way to search or organize it, and anything past the first few scrolls is effectively lost.

What it does
Search your whole Watch Later list. Find a saved video by title or channel, even in a list of thousands.
Categorize by topic and purpose. Group videos by what they are about (matched to your own playlists) and why you saved them: background, learning, entertainment, reference, or unclear.
Find duplicates. Spot videos you saved more than once or already placed in a playlist.
Clean up safely. Review finished, stale, and unavailable videos, then remove them. Every removal asks for your confirmation first.
Move videos into playlists. Accept suggested playlist matches and add videos to your own playlists, only after you confirm.
Privacy first
Your video list is stored only in your browser.
No analytics, no telemetry, no developer server.
Connecting a Google account is optional. It is needed only for duplicate detection and playlist moves.
